Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025 · Effective immediately

📋 Overview

AI Career Assistant is a Chrome Extension that helps you generate cover letters and analyze your LinkedIn profile using AI.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we access, what we don't collect, and how your information is handled.

✅ Short version: We don't collect, store, or sell any of your personal data. Ever.

🔑 API Key Storage

The extension requires a Groq API key to generate AI responses. This key is:

  • Stored locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage
  • Never transmitted to our servers or any third party
  • Never logged or recorded anywhere
  • Only used to make direct API calls from your browser to Groq

📄 Job Description Data

When you generate a cover letter, the job description text you enter is:

  • Sent directly from your browser to the Groq API
  • Not stored on our servers
  • Not shared with any third party other than Groq for AI processing
The extension communicates directly with Groq's API. Your data goes: Your Browser → Groq API. We are not in the middle.

🌐 Websites We Access

The extension only activates on these websites:

  • linkedin.com — to detect job descriptions and profile text
  • indeed.com — to detect job descriptions

We only read page content when you explicitly click "Auto-detect" or "Analyze". We do not monitor your browsing activity.

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, address)
  • No browsing history
  • No cookies or tracking
  • No analytics or usage data
  • No financial information
  • No location data

👶 Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
